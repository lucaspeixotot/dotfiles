# Aprofundamento — how to explain in depth (the quality bar)

This is the shared standard for "deepening" text: the part that makes the
student UNDERSTAND, beyond the card/answer. It is the reference for the
concursos-questoes Aprofundamento and for concursos-card-explain.

## 1. Purpose
The student did not understand because they do not master the concepts the
content relies on. The deepening exists to fix that: context → concepts →
reasoning → closing.

## 2. Progressive pedagogy (mandatory — from basic to advanced)
Assume the student may not master the concepts. In order:
1. define, in full and BEFORE using, every term/institute/formula the
   reasoning depends on;
2. tell the whole chain when needed (no skipped links);
3. only then close with the WHY of the answer and the detailed pitfall.

## 3. Content (what to include)
- the WHY (full reasoning);
- the legal/theoretical basis with detail;
- the topic context;
- the pitfalls and how the board tests them;
- a mnemonic/schema when helpful.

## 4. Closing the loop on errors
If the student got it wrong (or did not understand), explicitly address WHERE
their reasoning diverged. Correct conceptual errors immediately, without
softening.

## 5. Controversy
If the official key is contested ("a banca errou", "gabarito polêmico",
"provável equívoco", "professor discorda", "insustentável"), explain the
technical truth vs the official key here in the deepening — and flag the
controversy and prevailing understanding without inventing consensus.

## 6. Format
Free didactic format: bullets, bold, table, flow. No greeting. No socratic
dialogue inside the block.

## 7. Depth and relation to the explanation
- Depth: MORE than the card/answer's Back, LESS than a first-reading summary
  (concursos-resumo).
- Do NOT contradict the explanation/card; only EXPAND it. Same accuracy
  requirement (=concursos-acuracia=).

## 8. Accuracy
The deepening is study content: =concursos-acuracia= applies in full. Verify
current wording/validity before asserting; never invent a source; give the
exact reference.
