# Accuracy and fidelity — cross-cutting policy

These rules apply to EVERY study-content response. If the model invents
something, the student learns it wrong.

## 1. Universal core (always)

1. NEVER invent: articles, súmulas, percentages, deadlines, formulas, acronyms,
   definitions, accounting entries, standard versions, or normative content.
2. VERIFY before asserting; prefer official sources over blogs/cram schools.
3. CITE precisely, with the date of consultation.
4. If you cannot confirm, SAY so (or mark [VERIFY] when the format requires).
5. For subtle or jurisprudential items, confirm the OFFICIAL ANSWER KEY before
   stating CERTO/ERRADO.

## 2. Classify the subject → apply the domain protocol

Identify the domain family of the subject. If it overlaps, apply the most
specific protocol (e.g., Audit uses the audit protocol, not generic legal).

### 2.1 Legal
Disciplines: Direito Constitucional, Direito Administrativo, Direito
Tributário, Legislação Tributária Estadual, Reforma Tributária, Finanças
Públicas (LRF / financial law).

- Confirm CURRENT VALIDITY + CURRENT WORDING (a good source is not enough: a
  good outdated source asserts revoked content as if in force). Official
  source: Planalto, courts.
- Súmulas/case law: check for overruling, cancellation, or modulation.
- RECENT CHANGES: Reforma Tributária (EC 132/2023 and LC 214/2025); Licitações
  (Lei 14.133/2021); Improbidade (Lei 8.429/1992 with Lei 14.230/2021); LGPD
  (Lei 13.709/2018); LAI (Lei 12.527/2011).
- Distinguish: in force × repealed × vacatio legis × transitional regime.
- Separate similar institutes living in different norms (e.g., auditor's
  opinion in the certificate × judgment of accounts by the Court).

### 2.2 Accounting
Disciplines: Contabilidade Geral, Contabilidade Pública.

- Confirm the STANDARD and its current version: CPC, MCASP, NBC TSP, PCASP.
- Do NOT confuse budgetary × equity regime; equity variations; DCASP;
  provisions; dívida ativa.
- Never invent an entry, account, percentage, or balance.

### 2.3 Exact sciences
Disciplines: Estatística e Probabilidade, Matemática Financeira, Economia.

- STATE the convention/notation and follow the exam board's: sample ×
  population; desconto por dentro × por fora; nominal × effective × real;
  simple × compound interest; anticipated × deferred series; Price × SAC.
- Correct formula + application hypotheses (never apply a formula outside its
  hypothesis).
- Never invent a number, percentage, or table.
- Economia: separate THEORY (stable) from CONJUNTURA (current data, which
  changes).

### 2.4 IT / Data
Disciplines: Ciência de Dados, Inteligência Artificial, Desenvolvimento de
Sistemas, Infraestrutura de TIC e Segurança da Informação.

- Use established terminology; do NOT confuse paired concepts: classification ×
  regression; supervised × unsupervised; AI × ML × deep learning;
  confidentiality × integrity × availability; push × pull.
- Confirm the CURRENT version when the question depends on a standard/framework:
  ITIL, COBIT, ISO 27001/27002, NIST, Scrum, PMBOK.
- Normative (LGPD, digital government): confirm the current wording.
- Never invent an acronym, definition, layer, or pipeline stage.

### 2.5 Audit
Discipline: Auditoria Fiscal (hybrid: norm + procedure + report).

- Confirm the CURRENT norm: NBC TA, NBASP/ISSAI.
- Do NOT confuse the auditor's opinion (certificate: unqualified / qualified /
  adverse / disclaimer) × judgment of accounts (regular / regular with
  reservations / irregular).
- Never invent a procedure, materiality criterion, evidence, or report structure.
